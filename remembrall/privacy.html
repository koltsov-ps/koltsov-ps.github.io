Privacy Policy

Last updated July 2026

This policy covers the information collected or submitted through the Remembrall app.

CloudKit and iCloud

Remembrall uses Apple CloudKit to sync your data between your own Apple devices. Your data lives in your private CloudKit database, encrypted with an account-based key — I don't have access to it. I also don't have access to your Apple ID, only a unique identifier linked to this app alone; it can't be used to track you across other apps. For more, see Apple's privacy policy.

Personal Data

Personal data is information that can identify you — for instance, contact details you share when you write to me, such as your name, email address, or postal address.

Email and Communication

Remembrall lets you email me directly from the app. If you do — from there or anywhere else — the personal data you send is stored so I can reply.

Firebase Analytics

I collect anonymized usage statistics through Google Firebase, a service of Google LLC. This includes information about your use of the app and your IP address, transferred and stored on Google's servers in the US. I use it to understand how the app is used and to improve it, based on my legitimate interest in a needs-based design. Google may also pass this information to third parties where required by law. For more, see Google's privacy policy.

Data held in Firebase Analytics is kept for 14 months, after which only aggregated statistics remain.

Firebase Crashlytics

I use Firebase Crashlytics, also from Google LLC. If Remembrall crashes, Crashlytics sends me technical information about your device and the crash so I can find the cause and fix it. This data isn't personal data, and its use is based on my legitimate interest in keeping the app working correctly.

Your Consent

By using Remembrall, you consent to this Privacy Policy.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, I'll post the update on this page. Questions in the meantime? Email koltsov.ps@gmail.com.
