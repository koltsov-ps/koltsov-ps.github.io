Remembrall

Remember anything, on a schedule your brain actually respects.

A focused flashcard app built around spaced repetition. Make cards by hand or import a CSV — Remembrall decides when it's time to review again.

Features

Everything a flashcard app needs. Nothing it doesn't.

01
Spaced repetition
Every review is scheduled by how well you knew the card last time, so your time goes to what you're about to forget — not what you already know.
02
Smart CSV import
Drop in a two-column CSV file. Remembrall works out which column is the question and which is the answer, flags duplicates, and lets you merge or skip before anything's added.
03
Reads cards aloud
Built-in text-to-speech reads your cards to you — pick a voice and accent for each deck, in any language your device can speak.
04
Private iCloud sync
Cards sync across your devices through your own iCloud account. Remembrall has no server of its own and never sees your data.
05
Two moods, one app
Space is moody and dark; Lavender is bright and airy. Switch anytime from Settings.
06
Review, and move on
A calm review screen with one job: show you the next card at the right moment.
Themes

Same app, two moods.

Pick a mood from Settings and the whole app repaints — navigation, cards, empty states. Space is the moody dark default; Lavender keeps things bright and airy. This is the same import screen, for real, in both.
